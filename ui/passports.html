Every agent in the fleet has one governance record. Inside a trust domain the organisation controls it is held in identity mode — the agent card in the Trust registry. When an agent acts across a boundary the organisation does not control, the same record is presented in passport mode, with a stamp per jurisdiction: what it may do there, how it is overseen, who granted it, and when the grant expires. Risk tier follows the primer — the highest of agency, impact and reach sets the tier, each amplifier adds one, and a cross-jurisdiction boundary sets Tier 4 on its own. Oversight is stamped per jurisdiction.
