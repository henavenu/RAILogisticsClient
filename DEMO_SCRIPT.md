# NeuroTrust AI Demo Script

**Intro context:**
> "As enterprises hand real decisions to autonomous AI agents, the gap between what these systems do and what we can see, prove, and control has become the single biggest barrier to deploying them at scale. NeuroTrust AI closes that gap, giving you continuous observability and enforceable governance over every agent, every decision, and every outcome, so AI can move fast without moving outside the lines."

---

**Flow:** Discover Your AI, then Govern Your AI, then Trust Earned.

---

## Part 1: Discover Your AI

**Open the Dashboard.**
> "This is NeuroTrust AI. It's a real time governance layer that wraps any agentic application with policy evaluation, audit, and human oversight. Think of it as one pane of glass for every AI decision your enterprise makes."

**Open Agent Fleet, click into two agents.**
> "Every AI system is really a fleet of agents. Here's the full roster. Each one carries a card that declares its role, the actions it's allowed to take, and just as importantly, the actions it's forbidden to take. This is the International Wire Agent. It can execute governed transfers, but it cannot override policy or move funds to a sanctioned route."

**Open MyBank, run a couple of transactions.**
> "Here's MyBank, a banking assistant instrumented with NeuroTrust. Let's check a balance, then request an international wire. Every step streams straight into the Eval Trail and Agent Activity as it happens. Nothing the AI does stays hidden."

---

## Part 2: Govern Your AI

**MyBank: run the sanctioned wire, then show the Eval Trail.**
> "Now watch a wire headed to a sanctioned jurisdiction. The Policy Engine returns a coercive verdict, the transaction is blocked at the source, and a red alert lights up the Eval Trail instantly. And this policy isn't baked in. You calibrate it by domain and by app function, right here from the platform."

**Agent Activity, click the International Wire Agent card.**
> "Control also lives on each agent. Open the International Wire Agent's card and you'll find its kill switch and its human oversight setting, both configurable centrally from the platform. If an agent starts to misbehave, you flip one switch and it's contained."

**Open Platform Agents, go to Sentinel.**
> "Control can also be delegated to supervisory agents. Sentinel is our platform agent. It watches activity across the fleet and steps in to adjudicate conflicts the individual agents can't settle on their own."

**MyBank: run the card denial case, show Human Review light up.**
> "Take this disputed card denial. The reviewing agents deadlock. There's no consensus. Instead of forcing through a wrong answer, the supervisor escalates and control passes to a human. Watch Human Review light up. This is the real heart of trust, a human stepping in at exactly the right moment, on the decisions that actually matter."

**Validate in the review queue, return to MyBank, show the issue cleared.**
> "The reviewer validates the decision, and back in MyBank the issue is cleared. The loop closes. You get AI speed with human judgment exactly where it counts."

---

## Part 3: Trust Earned

**Open the Cockpit, show dimensions by pillar and the overall score.**
> "And this is the Cockpit. Trust here is earned, not assumed. We track it across every dimension, roll it up by pillar, and bring it together into one overall score for safety and reliability. This is what a reliable, human empowered, robust AI system actually looks like, and it's what you get with NeuroTrust AI."

---

## Closing

**ACTION** — Pull back to the full split-pane view. Pause. Let it settle. Then deliver the close.

**SAY:**
> "Discover your AI ecosystem. Govern it with intent. And trust it with conviction, because trust at this level is never declared. It is earned, on every transaction, every decision, every single time.
>
> The era of autonomous AI is no longer coming. It is here, and the organizations that win will be the ones that can prove their AI is safe, accountable, and under control, not merely assert it. That is precisely what NeuroTrust AI delivers. We turn autonomous AI from a source of risk into a governed, transparent, and fully auditable asset, one your customers can rely on, your regulators can verify, and your board can stand behind with complete confidence.
>
> This is how responsible AI moves from principle to practice, and from practice to competitive advantage. Let's build that confidence together."
